# Meditative Game Experience

A gentle, emotional journey through self-discovery, acceptance, and love. Guide a small sphere through five meaningful phases of transformation.

## Overview

This is a visual meditation experience with no sound, focusing on minimalist 3D aesthetics, smooth animations, and emotional storytelling through interaction and symbolism.

## The Five Phases

### 🩶 Phase 1 — Gray Beginning
- A small gray sphere with a sad face on a foggy plane
- Move your cursor to guide the sphere
- Leave faint trails that fade, symbolizing presence
- *"It's okay to feel lost."*

### 🎨 Phase 2 — Choosing Color
- The fog clears, revealing 5 glowing color spheres
- Hover over spheres to see them glow
- Click to absorb a color that feels like you
- *"Choose the color that feels like you."*

### 💞 Phase 3 — The Encounter
- A bright yellow sphere drifts toward you from the distance
- Approach it to merge and increase your color saturation
- Or stay still and let it pass peacefully
- *"Hope you feel better."* or *"Peace also lives in solitude."*

### 🌉 Phase 4 — The Bridge
- Cross a narrow glowing bridge hovering in white space
- Fall off and you'll gently respawn at the start
- *"If you fall, I'll catch you."*

### ☁️ Phase 5 — Reunion and Ascension
- Meet your gray past self at the end of the bridge
- Approach to merge and reunite
- Watch as the unified sphere ascends into light
- *"Can we hug?"*
- *"Well… I will be seeing you, perhaps again, someday."*
- *"I love you."*

## How to Run

1. Simply open `index.html` in a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. No build process or server required
3. Move your mouse/cursor to guide the sphere
4. Click to interact with color spheres in Phase 2

## Technical Details

- **Engine**: Three.js (r128)
- **Rendering**: WebGL with soft shadows
- **Camera**: Top-down perspective with slight angle
- **Interaction**: Mouse/cursor-based movement
- **Design**: Minimalist 3D with matte textures and soft lighting

## Visual Principles

- No sound or music (pure visual meditation)
- Clean 3D shapes with matte textures
- Smooth camera and object transitions
- Face rotates naturally with sphere movement (rolling effect)
- Text fades in/out gently
- Empty environments with fog and gradients

## Browser Compatibility

Works best in modern browsers with WebGL support:
- Chrome 90+
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+
- Edge 90+
